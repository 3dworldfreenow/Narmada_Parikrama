What the columns mean

No.
Serial number of the stop within the stage, as in the original guide. A blank number means the original row had no number.
Km / Total
Distance from the previous stop, and the running total from the start of that stage.
Stay
Where a parikramavasi can stay: ashram, dharamshala, sadhu's hut, etc.
Tirth
Sacred bathing place (tirth) at this spot.
Sangam
A river that joins the Narmada here.
Ghat
Whether there is a built (stone) ghat.
Bridge
Whether a bridge crosses the Narmada here (road, railway or foot). Parikramavasis must not cross it.
Path
Footpath, forest footpath, dirt road or paved road to the next stop.
Food
Cooked food (annakshetra / bhojan) available.
Rations
Sadavrat: dry rations given to cook yourself.
Halt
Number of halt days (mukkam) noted in the original guide for this stop.

Credit & source

Original work: “नर्मदा परिक्रमा”, a village-wise Narmada Parikrama route table in Marathi, made in three stages (टप्पा). The PDF's file details show it was created in January 2014 in CorelDRAW, with the author field “Swayam”. It does not name a publisher, and I could not find the original publisher online. All credit for the route, distances and facility details goes to the original compiler(s). If you know who made it, please tell the site admin so their name can be added here.

This English edition: the legacy Marathi font in the PDF was converted to Unicode text and translated into English. Village and temple names are transliterated into Roman script, so spellings may differ from local usage; the original Marathi name is shown under each village for checking.

Please note: the data dates from 2014. Ashrams, food arrangements, roads and boat timings change, and some places are now under dam water. Confirm locally before relying on any entry.
